HOW TO PLAY

Colors shift through 10 different hues, changing every 30 seconds.

Eat matching orbs to grow. Mismatched enemies shrink you.

Lives: you start with 5 ❤️. Every wrong-colored orb costs one life — lose them all and it's game over.

Danger: shrink to minimum size and your snake dies!

Overload: eat two different colors within a few seconds for temporary golden invincibility (5s).

Duel: collide with a mismatched enemy and you get one timing-based chance to survive instead of instant death.

Prism orbs explode into a burst of orbs when eaten and give a big growth + score boost.

Star ★ gives double score for 7s. Magnet 🧲 pulls nearby orbs to you for 5s.

Controls:
Mouse / Touch: steer toward cursor
Arrow Keys / WASD: steer
Space: boost / trigger duel
Escape: pause

Your snake moves in a straight line when idle. Hitting your own body is safe!
