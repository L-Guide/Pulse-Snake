PULSE SNAKE
Color-shift survival. Grow your snake, dodge enemies, survive the pulse.

HOW TO PLAY

Colors shift through 10 different hues, changing every 30 seconds.

Eat matching orbs to grow. Mismatched enemies are lethal on contact.

Overload: eat two different colors within a few seconds for temporary golden invincibility.

Duel: collide with a mismatched enemy and you get one timing-based chance to survive instead of instant death.

Prism orbs explode into a burst of orbs when eaten.

Controls:
Mouse / Touch: steer toward cursor
Arrow Keys / WASD: steer
Space: boost / trigger duel
Escape: pause

Your snake moves in a straight line when idle. Hitting your own body is safe!
